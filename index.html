Lorenzo Palazzetti

Postdoctoral Researcher, Penn State  ·  Habilitated Associate Professor in Informatics

My core work is on optimization algorithms and path planning for drones, together with IoT and computer vision for monitoring. I also follow emerging topics in the field. At Penn State I build AI- and sensor-driven monitoring systems for livestock farms.

What I'm working on

  • BVLoS drone operations

    Routing and planning algorithms that let drones fly safely beyond the pilot's visual line of sight. This is the topic of my INdAM–GNCS research project.

  • Efficient computer vision for smart agriculture

    Vision models for orchards and livestock that run on embedded devices, keeping energy use and computation low.

  • Drone localization and navigation

    Locating and guiding drones when GNSS is unreliable or unavailable, using onboard sensing and radio ranging.

The questions I work on

Algorithms & optimization

How do you plan routes and schedules when energy, wind and time all push back? Exact, approximation and heuristic answers to hard combinatorial problems.

Autonomous drones

How can a drone deliver, scout or inspect safely, even far from the pilot and without GPS?

Computer vision

How small and fast can a model be and still spot a pest, an animal or a gesture on embedded hardware?

IoT for agriculture

How do we turn orchards and barns into places that report their own condition, through sensors, drones and edge devices?
