Postdoctoral Researcher

Lorenzo
Palazzetti

Pennsylvania State University  ·  Computer Science

I design algorithms for problems that have to survive contact with the physical world — drones that must land before the battery dies, sensors that must be reached in one pass, orchards that must be scouted before the pests spread. PhD with distinction (Florence, 2024); now at Penn State leading work on AI- and sensor-driven monitoring for livestock and agriculture, after research on BVLOS autonomous drone mobility in GPS-denied environments. My agenda spans combinatorial optimization, autonomous systems, computer vision, edge computing and the Internet of Things.
