Postdoctoral Researcher

Lorenzo
Palazzetti

University of Perugia  ·  Computer Science

I earned my B.S. and M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Perugia (2018, 2020) and a Ph.D. in Computer Science with distinction from the University of Florence (2024). After my Ph.D., I held a HALY.ID Research Fellowship and later a Postdoctoral position at the University of Perugia, where I worked on IoT- and computer vision–based systems for insect monitoring and conducted research on Beyond Visual Line of Sight (BVLOS) autonomous drone mobility, focusing on vision-based perception, navigation in GPS-denied environments, and multi-sensor integration. I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at The Pennsylvania State University, leading research on AI- and sensor-driven systems for intelligent livestock and agricultural monitoring. My research lies at the intersection of algorithm design, combinatorial optimization, autonomous systems, computer vision, and the Internet of Things (IoT), with the goal of developing scalable, real-world solutions for complex environments.
